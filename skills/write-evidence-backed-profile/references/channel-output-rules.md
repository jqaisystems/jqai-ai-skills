# Channel Output Rules

Select the smallest format that fully answers the request.

## Short Bio

- Lead with current work and the clearest supported distinction.
- Use one or two concrete proof points.
- Respect the exact character or word limit.

## Long Bio or About Copy

- Connect current focus, relevant background, representative work, and intended audience.
- Keep chronology only when it explains the present positioning.
- Avoid turning every available fact into a list.

## Application Response

- Answer the exact field first.
- Connect evidence to the stated requirement.
- Explain partial fit honestly and omit email framing unless requested.

## Portfolio Introduction

- Explain the type of problems addressed, the working approach, and representative evidence.
- Do not imply work was completed for a client unless the evidence confirms that relationship and public use is approved.

## Proposal Introduction

- Reflect the audience's stated need, then establish relevant evidence and the practical approach.
- Do not invent scope, pricing, timing, approval, or outcomes.

## Cover Note or Outreach

- Include recipient fields only when supplied.
- Keep the connection specific and supported.
- Return a draft for human review. Never send it.

## Final Channel Check

Confirm length, tone, requested fields, factual grounding, link accuracy, and the intended call to action. Remove unsupported claims before polishing style.
