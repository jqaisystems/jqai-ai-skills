# Media Credits and Editorial Dates

## Distinct dates

Record source publication, event, video publication, article publication, and article editorial update dates separately. Do not infer one from another. Keep a date-only source as a calendar date; do not fabricate a time or shift it across timezones. If a source supplies an instant, retain its offset and document any requested display conversion.

The default article publication date describes the article, not its oldest source. An explicit alternative editorial policy may use a primary video's verified date, but label that convention and avoid implying the article existed before it did. Unknown publication dates remain unknown or planned; do not silently replace them with today's date. An editorial update date reflects a real revision.

For a video-led article, verify the primary video's date from its authoritative page or approved evidence. If unavailable, retain the unknown source date and ask only when the missing date blocks the requested output. A draft may continue with a visible unresolved check.

## Credits and visuals

List the primary embed and supplemental embeds separately, with creator, direct source, verified date or date uncertainty, and the claim or demonstration each supports.

The player already supplies a thumbnail. Do not add an identical static image solely to repeat it. Explanatory screenshots, diagrams, comparisons, and original visuals can still be useful. Social-preview/Open Graph images are separate assets and must not be discarded as duplicate in-article thumbnails.

Credit contributing creators and distinguish known usage permission from unresolved rights questions. Attribution alone does not establish permission. If the user has not supplied approved media, return an asset brief or omission rather than importing third-party material into a public package.

## Verification and handoff

Check title, slug, description, date labels, credits, embed identity, image purpose, and Link Map. Keep unsupported statements out of the finished factual copy. Record date and credit questions in the verification report.

This skill prepares editorial artifacts only. Website registries, structured data, routes, and deployment belong to a separately authorized website implementation and verification workflow.
