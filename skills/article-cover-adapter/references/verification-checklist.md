# Adaptation verification

## Before editing

Record exact wording, visible credits, brand elements, and protected regions. Validate that the target dimensions and ratio agree. Safe margins are editorial choices unless verified platform guidance establishes them.

For wide artwork, do not center-crop lettering away. For tall artwork, preserve the complete protected composition and extend/reframe the surrounding canvas. If a local background extension cannot preserve the design cleanly, return the limitation for review instead of inventing replacement artwork.

## Export checks

- Source remains byte-identical.
- Derivative matches requested dimensions, ratio, and format.
- Exact text and punctuation, logos, credits, and important artwork remain intact.
- Protected content remains inside agreed safe margins at full size and small display sizes.
- Colour, transparency, orientation, and animation are preserved where relevant; disclose intentional adaptation changes.
- No additional purchase or external upload occurred without separate approval.

## Metadata-only cleanup

Save the adapted pre-clean export privately. Inspect targeted generator/provenance fields, apply a format-aware selective operation, and inspect again. Compare encoded content and decoded rendering between this pre-clean export and the cleaned derivative. A dimension-changing adaptation is not expected to be pixel-identical to the original artwork.

Preserve unrelated authorship, copyright, ICC data, orientation and animation. Keep provenance notes separate. Unknown or malformed metadata structures require a safe handler or an unverified result, not wholesale metadata removal.

Only claim which supported fields were removed. Do not claim undetectability, removal of platform-applied labels, or exemption from disclosure requirements.

## Preview and handoff

A local thumbnail mockup is local evidence only. Record the exact platform surface and date if an authorized real preview is inspected; other surfaces may crop differently. If unavailable, mark platform preview pending. Never claim a proof sample was uploaded merely because its dimensions passed.

List unresolved checks before asking for approval of the exact finished package.
