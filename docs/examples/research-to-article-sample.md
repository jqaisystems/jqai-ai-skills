## Draft Excerpt

An approval gate gives a reviewer a defined point to accept, revise, or reject an automated output before it reaches a public destination. That does not guarantee accuracy, but it makes responsibility and intervention visible.

For a small team, one review point before the highest-risk action is often a more practical starting position than approval at every step. This is a recommendation based on the fictional workflow described here, not a universal benchmark.
