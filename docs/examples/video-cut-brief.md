# Paper Signals: approved review brief

Fictional edition A. The six-second cut has three consecutive two-second cards. Each card must display its exact word:

1. **OPEN** from 0 to 2 seconds.
2. **FOLD** from 2 to 4 seconds.
3. **PLACE** from 4 to 6 seconds.

Keep the visible credit **Made by Example Studio** on screen throughout the PLACE card. At the start of PLACE, sound one short electronic tone, with no tone on the other cards. There is no narration requirement.

If reviewing the companion grid, read the three samples in chronological order from left to right. The samples are not timecoded.
