# Write Evidence-Backed Profile Sample

This fictional example drafts portfolio copy for Example Builder from a limited evidence pack.

## Approved Evidence

- Three public workflow projects are listed at `https://example.com/work`.
- A public article dated 2026-04-12 explains a review-first automation approach.
- The supplied profile confirms current freelance availability.
- No source supports ten years of automation experience, enterprise clients, or measured revenue outcomes.

## Requested Positioning

`An enterprise automation expert with ten years of experience and proven revenue growth.`

## Evidence Map

| Requested claim | State | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Builds reviewable automation workflows. | supported | Use with the public projects and article. |
| Enterprise automation expert. | unsupported | Omit. No enterprise evidence was supplied. |
| Ten years of automation experience. | unsupported | Omit. No dated experience record supports it. |
| Proven revenue growth. | unsupported | Omit. No approved metric or outcome supports it. |
| Currently available for freelance work. | supported | Use because the current profile confirms it. |

## Finished Portfolio Introduction

Example Builder creates reviewable automation workflows for small teams. Three public projects demonstrate a practical approach built around clear handoffs, human approval points, and checked outputs. Example Builder is currently available for focused freelance projects.

## Omitted Claims

Enterprise experience, ten years of experience, and revenue growth were excluded because the evidence pack does not support them.
