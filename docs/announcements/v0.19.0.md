# v0.19.0: Video Cut Reviewer

This candidate adds [Video Cut Reviewer](../../skills/video-cut-reviewer/SKILL.md) as the library's 53rd skill. Give it the approved brief and one selected cut or frame grid. It returns an editorial review, not a replacement cut or permission to publish.

In [one cut, two reviews](../examples/video-cut-sample.md), the shot order is wrong even though the video is the requested length. See what survives a second review with only three stills.
